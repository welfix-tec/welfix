Questions people ask first

How long does a website take?

Usually 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the number of pages, how complex it is, and how quickly we get your content and feedback.

How long does a custom operations system take?

It depends on the scope. After the first consultation we give you a written plan with the timeline and cost before any work starts. We show you working parts early, so there are no surprises at the end.

Do you support us after launch?

Yes. We offer support and maintenance plans that keep your website or system up to date, secure and running well.

Can we update the content ourselves?

Yes. We build with simple content management and train your team, so you can change text, prices and products without technical knowledge.

How do you measure social media results?

Engagement rate, follower growth, traffic from social media to your site, and conversions. You get a report every month.

Do you work outside Kigali?

Yes. We work with businesses across Rwanda and Uganda, and remotely beyond.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us the problem. We'll recommend the simplest fix, even if it's a small one.
