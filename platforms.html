For logistics and transport companies

Fleet Guard

Trucks, drivers, safety checks and orders usually live in different notebooks, chats and spreadsheets. Fleet Guard puts them in one place, so the operations team knows what's moving, who's driving and what's overdue.

  • Truck register with status and availability
  • Driver records and assignments
  • HSE (health, safety and environment) checks before each trip
  • Orders tracked from received to delivered

For companies with several departments

MyRequest

Money requests usually travel by paper forms, emails and WhatsApp, and get stuck on someone's desk. With MyRequest, an employee from any department submits a request, their manager approves it, and finance pays it. Everyone sees where it is.

  • Requests from every department in one place
  • Manager approval, then finance payment
  • Status visible to the person who asked
  • A full record for audits and budgets

Cura Menu

Guests scan the QR code on their table, browse your dishes and prices, and send their order straight to you without waiting to catch a waiter. Built-in ad space lets you promote specials or sell it to partners.

  • QR code for every table
  • Self-ordering with the table number attached
  • Update dishes and prices anytime
  • Ad space for specials and partner brands

For gyms, clubs and training centres

Gym QR Login

Paper sign-in sheets get lost, and nobody can tell you how many members came last month. With Gym QR Login, members scan in at the door, and each visit lands in a Google Sheet you already know how to use.

  • One QR code per member
  • Check-ins logged with name and time
  • Data in Google Sheets, with no new software to learn
  • See busy hours and regular members at a glance
