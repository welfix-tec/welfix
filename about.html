Most businesses don't need more technology. They need the gap in their day fixed.

We look for the place where work waits, repeats or gets lost, and that's where we build.

Welfix started in Kigali with a simple observation: many businesses still run important work on paper, phone calls and scattered spreadsheets. Orders get missed, customers wait, and managers can't see what's happening until it's too late.

So we started building the fixes ourselves. Fleet Guard came from watching logistics teams juggle trucks, drivers and safety checks by hand. Cura Menu came from guests waiting to order in busy restaurants. Gym QR Login came from sign-in sheets nobody could count. MyRequest came from staff chasing signatures to get a payment approved.

Today we work with businesses of every size, from small shops getting their first website to large companies connecting whole operations. The approach stays the same: understand the work first, then build the simplest digital solution that makes it run better.

There's no limit to what we'll take on. Wherever there's a gap, there's an opportunity for Welfix to step in.
